# Evidence: City states 2.0 and the arena generators on the terrain catalogue

Game-view renders (`glob2 map render`, downscaled from 4096 to 2048 pixels) of every rebuilt
generator in every climate, 256x256 with 4 colonies. `renders/overview.jpg` is the grid:
rows City states, Carousel, Switchbacks, Gauntlet, Amphitheatre; columns temperate, arid,
highland, volcanic. Seeds (temperate/arid/highland/volcanic): City states 2/1/3/4,
Carousel 4/3/1/2, Switchbacks 9/2/1/5, Gauntlet 4/2/1/12, Amphitheatre 3/5/1/2.
