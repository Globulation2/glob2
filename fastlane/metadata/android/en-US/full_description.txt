Globulation 2 is a real time strategy game built around assigning units to tasks rather than directing each unit individually.

Build a colony, manage resources, play against computer opponents, and create maps in the editor. The Android edition uses the same game sources and SDL renderer as the desktop edition.

Play with others on your local network, or online at app.glob2online.com: rooms with invite links, quick match, ratings and match history, against players on Android, iOS, desktop and the browser. Online play runs on the project's own free-software server and starts with a guest account that needs no e-mail address; you can link a sign-in method later and delete your account from the game's settings. Single-player games, the campaign, the map editor and LAN games need no account and send no data to the project.

No ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking.
