Adapted from "Apple Cider" by Zane Little Music (CC0 1.0), a cozy autumn piece at
about 99 bpm. No stems were published, so Demucs (htdemucs, 4 stems, shifts=2)
estimates drums, bass, other and vocals from the mix; each mood moves those stems
relative to the original mix, which keeps the separator's residual everywhere.

Changes made (not required by CC0, recorded for provenance):
- Loop: 40 bars of the source, 29.548-126.264 s (96.7 s), with a 10 ms crossfade
  at the wrap from the source's own continuation.
- calm: drums at -20 dB and low-passed at 900 Hz (a faint pulse that also masks
  separation gaps), bass -5 dB, other and vocals -1 dB with the other stem
  low-passed at 7 kHz.
- building: drums -7 dB, bass -1.5 dB.
- combat: the original mix with drums +3.5 dB, bass +1.5 dB and a +1.5 dB shelf
  above 4 kHz.
- Mastered by the shared pipeline (-18/-17/-16 LUFS, true-peak limited).

Separation (measured on the loop): reconstruction residual -31.5 dB; drum-onset
correlation in the non-drum remainder 0.39 against 0.19 for real stems, so faint
drum ghosts are expected in calm. Demucs is MIT; its weights come from the
Hugging Face repository adefossez/HTDemucs and are a tool only.

Listening verdict: approved by the maintainer by ear.
