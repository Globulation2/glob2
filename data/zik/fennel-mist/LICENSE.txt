An original slow pastoral score in E Dorian (4/4, 72 bpm, 24 bars): bassoon and horn under a flute descant, with harp and strings. Calm is the low lead, descant, harp and quiet pads; building adds semiquaver harp, pizzicato and soft hand drums; combat hands the tune to horn over a semiquaver spiccato engine, tremolo violas, tuba and a drum line.

Built by tools/music/sets/fennel-mist in the Globulation 2 source tree.
