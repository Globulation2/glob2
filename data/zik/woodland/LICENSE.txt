Adapted from the Level theme of JC Sounds' "Woodland Music - Vol 1" (130 bpm,
B-flat minor), using the composer's own eight "Main Loop" stems: Choir, E Drums 1,
Perc, Strings, WWind, Xperiments, Xtra Inst and Xtra Perc. The stems sum to the
published master (residual -38 dB), so the moods are plain weighted sums of real
stems on the composer's loop.

Changes made (CC BY 4.0 requires saying so):
- The 78-bar (144.0 s) loop is shortened to 60 bars (110.8 s) by removing bars
  32-49, an 18-bar block (a multiple of the piece's 3-bar drum cycle), with a 12 ms
  crossfade just before the bar line. Of every 14-30-bar block, this one joined
  best (per-stem activity and chroma compared on both sides of the cut).
- calm: woodwinds, choir and extra instruments; strings -4 dB low-passed at 4 kHz,
  the synth texture -3 dB low-passed at 2.5 kHz, the electronic drums reduced to a
  faint 500 Hz-low-passed pulse at -19 dB; no percussion.
- building: electronic drums at -3 dB, light orchestral percussion (-9 dB,
  low-passed at 5 kHz) and the extra percussion layer at -6 dB.
- combat: the full arrangement with the electronic drums +3 dB, percussion +5 dB,
  extra percussion +4 dB, woodwinds -2 dB and a +2 dB shelf above 4 kHz.
- Mastered by the shared pipeline (-18/-17/-16 LUFS, true-peak limited).

Listening verdict: approved by the maintainer by ear.
