An original folk waltz in D major (3/4, 126 bpm, 48 bars) for alto and tenor recorder, ocarina, kalimba, folk harp and hand drums with strings. An 8+8 tune, a B minor middle on ocarina, the tune an octave up at the climax, and a quiet coda through bVII and bVI. Calm is lead, rolled harp and kalimba; building adds a pizzicato waltz rhythm, marimba and frame drum; combat adds spiccato strings, horn, timpani and darbuka.

Built by tools/music/sets/thistle-waltz in the Globulation 2 source tree.
