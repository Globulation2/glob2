An original chamber score in G minor (4/4, 92 bpm, 32 bars) for flute, clarinet, oboe, harp, glockenspiel and strings, echoing the key, tempo and high woodwind lead of the original soundtrack. A theme, a development in Bb, a G major lift with a chromatic-mediant climax, and a varied return. Calm is lead, counter-line and rolled harp; building adds pizzicato, walking bass and hand percussion; combat adds spiccato strings, horn, timpani and a light drum line.

Built by tools/music/sets/moss-lanterns in the Globulation 2 source tree.
