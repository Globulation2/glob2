[AICastor-Profile]
Core strategy: Develops conservatively and handles maps with few resources well.\n\nStrengths: Restrained growth suits scarce-resource matches.\n\nCounterplay to try: Claim space early; do not assume that scarce resources will cripple it.
[AICastor-Summary]
Conservative growth; comfortable with scarce resources.
[AICortex-Profile]
Core strategy: Manages food supply, expands toward fresh wheat, and gathers successive warrior waves. Forward inns and hospitals support distant attacks.\n\nStrengths: Grouped reinforcements, sustained pressure, and responses to food shortages.\n\nCounterplay to try: Contest fresh wheat and forward support; apply pressure before its waves form.\n\nExperimental AI. Medium difficulty; counterplay suggestions are not benchmark ratings.
[AICortex-Summary]
Food-aware growth and supported attack waves.
[AINicowar-Profile]
Core strategy: Grows large quickly and sends huge armies at its opponents.\n\nStrengths: Rapid expansion supports aggressive military pressure.\n\nCounterplay to try: Contest its expansion and use terrain to limit how much of its army can engage.
[AINicowar-Summary]
Rapid expansion into large, aggressive armies.
[AINone-Profile]
Core strategy: Issues no AI orders.\n\nStrengths: A predictable idle opponent for experiments.\n\nWeaknesses: Does not expand or adapt.
[AINone-Summary]
An idle colony for experiments.
[AINumbi-Profile]
Core strategy: Expands lightly and quickly. A gentler opponent for practicing colony management.\n\nStrengths: Gives you room to learn economy and combat together.\n\nCounterplay to try: Build a stronger economy and trained army before committing to an attack.
[AINumbi-Summary]
Light, swift expansion.
[AIReachToInfinity-Profile]
Core strategy: Does not attack, but builds enormous bases and aggressively converts units.\n\nStrengths: Growth and conversion create pressure without conventional attacks.\n\nCounterplay to try: Use the opening to develop, and avoid feeding units into conversion.
[AIReachToInfinity-Summary]
Huge bases and conversion instead of attacks.
[AIWarrush-Profile]
Core strategy: Attacks quickly and punishingly, before opponents finish their buildup.\n\nStrengths: Punishes undefended expansion and slow military preparation.\n\nCounterplay to try: Survive the first attack while maintaining your economy, then counterattack.

Fast attacks that test your early defenses.
