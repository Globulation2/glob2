Prepared candidate, NOT BUILT/TESTED/MEASURED.

Rejected first proposal: unconditional notify_one in completeJob/settle is not sufficient to preserve the spare-worker presentation latency guarantee. A waiter selected for the single notification may be the designated presentation worker with simulationClaims>=8, which deliberately prioritizes a pending presentation chunk. A spare non-designated waiter could remain asleep while a required successor waits behind that chunk. Being able to claim a Group does not imply that the fairness branch will choose it first.

Prepared replacement uses notifyDeferredReady(), called only under mutex at lane completion and continuation requeue. Broadcast when presentation || presentationPending is nonnull; otherwise single-wake. No new CVs, no lane-issued guard, clocks, hardware thresholds or topology assumptions. Initial multi-job admission, run generations, shutdown, presentation submission/completion and service registration retain all existing broadcasts. Completion CVs retain all existing broadcasts.

At notification snapshot with no presentation, worker() chooses required work before WorkerOnly service: the service branch is reached only after claim(false) returns invalid. Designated presentation worker cannot enter WorkerOnly (maintenanceClaimable excludes it), and every other worker also claims required work before that service. A worker already inside a bounded service or presentation callback is not waiting on ready and cannot consume notify_one. A later presentation admission broadcasts separately, so it cannot silently introduce pending presentation into this single-wake case without waking spare workers. run() admission likewise retains its broadcast/generation protocol. Each completed lane ordinal enables <=1 immediate successor; each settled continuation requeues one index. Priority arbitration/predicates/claims and state transition remain under the original mutex. Stored ready state survives notifications with zero sleepers.

Optional observer limitation: final lane completion with no successor may previously have provided every maintenance-eligible worker an incidental opportunity; a single arbitrary wake can select the designated worker and miss that optional idle-tail opportunity. The existing documented service contract allows tail observations to remain pending until another opportunity/disposal. Registration broadcast is unchanged. Do not claim identical opportunistic sampling or GPU-policy measurements: required CRC/deadlines/lifetime/fallback guards and live GPU health must be checked. Do not use this as permission to relax device terminal proofs, cancellation, lifetime or publication rules.

Strongest focused black-box regression (prepared test design, not yet implemented/run):
A. N3,N5 (and larger N) with TWO different lanes, suspended predecessor callbacks whose external completion tickets are retained. Wait until both tickets are published; keep successors submitted with later valid due keys and verify neither can run. Start one deliberately gated presentation chunk and confirm it is entered on the designated worker. The designated worker remains busy until the test releases it. From a separate producer, resume both tickets with continuations (include a nonzero group offset) and complete another independent ticket. Join resumed batches/successors and unrelated required work BEFORE releasing presentation. Assert per-lane continuation-before-successor order, no duplicates, ownerJobs==0, all required work completes while presentation gate remains closed, captures stay leased until callbacks end, joinAll succeeds, then release/cancel and cleanly stop. Watchdog releases test gates on failure so a regression fails instead of hanging. Required progress must not rely on submitting a new batch after external resolution (that would introduce a broadcast and conceal lost wakes).
B. Same suspension/resume chain with a deliberately gated WorkerOnly pass already executing on a normal worker and NO presentation. Verify the designated worker completes required continuations/successors BEFORE the service gate is released; this tests its maintenance exclusion and required-work priority. With several workers, leave another normal worker spare and ensure all required joins complete without service completion. Then release service, dispose, reconfigure and verify no retained handles/tickets. An already executing optional callback isn't interruptible; don't require its own worker to preempt it.
C. Preserve existing presentation-fairness test (a continuous required backlog must not starve presentation), one-worker test (one chunk may delay a join), and spare-worker test (presentation cannot hold all required completion). Exercise pending/replaced/canceled/resumable presentation plus external ticket settlement with multi-lane backlog. Pending-fairness waiter selection cannot be forced by public APIs without adding a test hook; repeat counterbalanced stress interleavings and retain the conditional source invariant instead of asserting a specific thread chosen by condition_variable.
D. Single worker (N2), owner-only (N1), thread creation failure, lane error->successor, early ticket completion while callback still running, duplicate resolution, destruction-retained tickets, nonzero continuation index, due order/horizon, multiple independent resumed jobs and unrelated blocking run generation. Use exact order/count/lifetime invariants, no scheduler-selection promises.

Whole existing ComputeExecutor suite is required across supported thread libraries/browser variants. Before adoption: broad release per-tick CRC comparisons (maps/sizes/AI counts/swim and CPU/GPU paths), save/load continuation, replay seek/save/native wire compatibility, GPU cancellation/quarantine/commit health; CPU/sysCPU/context switches + processwall by map/count/native-feature strata.
