Minimal proposed experiment:
One atomic typed health: Ready, CancellationSuspended, PermanentFailure.
Serialize admission and mask publication/recovery under existing failureMutex.
Cancellation does not poison BackendSession.failed. Permanent failures and
already-failed sessions are never reset. No new broker/context/generation layer.
After ordinary cancellation of previously unambiguous accepted commands,
attempt at most one optional same-in-order-queue trailing marker event and flush.
Track it through unchanged proof.enqueue; never clear ambiguous state or call
drained merely for an enqueue. Missing marker/event, enqueue/flush/query errors
and negative status keep permanent fail-stop. Existing events can prove only
their covered prefix. No clFinish; background production DeviceService owns
recovery maintenance. Public synchronous backend calls retain caller responsibility;
no universal owner-free or bounded driver-call latency claim.
Only the existing background reaper can restore the compiled mask after ALL
retained transfers have positive proof, zero active lanes and healthy kernel.
Canceled GPU outputs remain abandoned; exact CPU fallback owns originals once.
Budgets remain64slots,64MiB shared host,128MiB perDevice and charged until proof.

Rejected broader alternative:
Creating new Device/context epochs would need TLS-lane invalidation, old reaper
