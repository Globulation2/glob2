Adapted from "Curious Critters (Extended Version)" by Matthew Pablo (CC BY 3.0), a
cute, playful orchestral piece at 101 bpm in F major. No stems were published, so
Demucs estimates them: htdemucs (4 stems) for drums and bass, htdemucs_6s for the
"other" (sustained and melodic) layer used to build calm. CC BY 3.0 requires credit,
a link to the licence and an indication of changes; the changes follow.

Changes made:
- Loop: 44 bars of the source, 16.324-120.878 s (104.55 s), with a 30 ms crossfade
  at the wrap from the source's own continuation.
- calm: only the 6-stem "other" layer (plus its "vocals" estimate at -6 dB),
  high-passed at 160 Hz and low-passed at 2.8 kHz, over a 4.5 s synthetic reverb
  bed made from that same layer (-3 dB), with slow gain riding; the ostinato, bass
  and drums are removed.
- building: the original mix with its percussion at -6 dB, plus a synthesised
  quarter-note shaker and frame drum on beats 2 and 4, 11 dB under the music.
- combat: the original mix with drums +6 dB, bass +3 dB and +4 dB below 90 Hz, plus
  synthesised taiko and timpani (tuned F2/C2) on beats 1 and 3, frame-drum and shaker
  eighths and three soft taiko swells every 8 bars, 3.5-4 dB under the music;
  stereo widening x1.3 above 300 Hz, a +2 dB tilt above 3.5 kHz and a 12 kHz
  low-pass.
- Mastered by the shared pipeline (-18/-17/-16 LUFS, true-peak limited), with calm
  1 dB under the shared target as in the version approved by ear ([master] below).

Added material: all percussion (taiko, timpani, frame drum, shaker) and all reverbs
are synthesised by glob2music.adapt (no samples, soundfonts or third-party impulse
responses) and are the Globulation 2 project's own work.

Separation (measured on the loop): reconstruction residual -27.6 dB (htdemucs) and
-15.2 dB (htdemucs_6s). Demucs is MIT; its weights come from the Hugging Face
repositories adefossez/HTDemucs and adefossez/HTDemucs-6s and are a tool only.

Listening verdict: approved by the maintainer by ear, after an earlier adaptation
with less contrast was judged too uniform across moods.
