# Savannah review previews

Native map-preview captures from the bulk study and the first playtest round. The complete
study (requests, reports, raw job archives, the eight saved sample maps, the playtest saves and
replays and the analysis scripts) lives on the
[evidence/savannah branch](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2/blob/evidence/savannah/artifacts/savannah/SUMMARY.md), kept out of the main source history.

The `*-preview.png` files are the bulk study's sample cases (map size, colony count and
pond setting in the name); `local-seed*-final.png` are the end states of the two local
playtest games.
