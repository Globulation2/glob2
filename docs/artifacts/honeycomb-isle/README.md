# Honeycomb isle revision 1 review previews

A city of hexagon blocks on an island in a lagoon, cut in two by a river; wheat fields on the edges,
ruins inside. The design, controls and verification are in
[HONEYCOMB_ISLE.md](../../map-generators/HONEYCOMB_ISLE.md).

- `revision1-256-4-seeds-2-6-10-23.png`: 256x256, four colonies, seeds 2, 6, 10 and 23 (walled
  cellar and station, well yard and cathedral, garden court and stadium, well yard and stadium).
- `revision1-256-8-seed5.png`: 256x256, eight colonies, seed 5 (blocks shrunk to fit).
- `revision1-512-6-seed6.png`: 512x512, six colonies, seed 6.
- `revision1-256-4-squares-seed7.png`: square blocks, 256x256, four colonies, seed 7.
- `revision1-home-zoom-seed10.png`: colony 0's home at seed 10, 8 pixels per tile.
- `revision1-centre-zoom-seed10.png`: the middle of the seed 10 city (stadium, ruins, river and a
  bridge), 8 pixels per tile.

Previews are the native map preview (`--generate-map ... --preview`), not in-game screenshots.
