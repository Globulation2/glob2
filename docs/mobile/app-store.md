Globulation 2. Keep claims aligned with the iOS build being submitted. TestFlight
approval is separate from App Store review; the store version remains on manual
release until the maintainer chooses to publish it.

**Description**

Globulation 2 is a real-time strategy game about guiding a colony rather than
directing every unit. Set priorities, choose where to build, and assign globs to
work. Your globs carry out those jobs while you focus on the shape of your
settlement and your next move.

Grow your economy, explore the map, and adapt your strategy as other colonies
expand. Play a single-player game against computer opponents, try the included
maps and campaigns, or create a map of your own.

On iPhone and iPad, touch controls let you navigate the world and manage your
colony. Single-player play does not require an account. The game is free and
open source.

**Version notes for the first public iOS version**

Globulation 2 comes to iPhone and iPad with touch controls, single-player
strategy gameplay, maps, campaigns, and a map editor.

**App Review notes**

No account is needed to start a single-player game. From the main menu, choose a
single-player game or campaign, select a map, and play with touch controls.
Network play is optional; local network permission is requested only when using
local multiplayer. Please test landscape and portrait layouts, save/load, and
background/restore.

## Artwork and screenshots

The iOS app icon is generated by `mobile/icons.py` into
`mobile/ios/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`. It composites the existing blue
Glob artwork onto an opaque medium-purple background, brighter than the Android
launcher tile so the background remains distinct from black at small sizes.
Check the compiled icon in the
archive as well as the PNG source; Apple optimizes bundled PNG files, so run
`xcrun pngcrush -revert-iphone-optimizations` before inspecting one extracted
from an IPA. Do not upload a transparent icon or a mock icon that differs from
the app bundle.

Capture screenshots from the release candidate running on the newest supported
iPhone and iPad simulators or physical devices. Use actual gameplay and UI,
without invented features or desktop screenshots. Select scenes that show:

1. A readable colony overview with workers, buildings, and the game UI —
   “Lead a living colony.”
2. A close view of assigning work and building a settlement —
   “Set jobs. Let globs work.”
3. Exploration or a later-game encounter with another colony —
   “Outthink rival colonies.”
4. The map or scenario selection screen — “New worlds to explore.”
5. The map editor, if its iOS touch behavior passes a play test —
   “Create your own maps.”

These short captions are optional overlays; if used, keep them outside essential
UI and compare the result to an unaltered capture. The first three screenshots
need to communicate the core game quickly. Apple
requires at least one and accepts up to ten per supported device size. Screenshots
must be JPEG or PNG with no alpha. Use the exact pixel sizes in [Apple's current
screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/).
App previews are optional. Keep raw captures in ignored `artifacts/` until the
final set has been reviewed, then place only the selected store assets in
`mobile/ios/store/` so the source repository preserves what was submitted.

## Submission checks

- Play the candidate on an iPhone and iPad; verify touch controls, save/load,
  backgrounding, and screenshots against the exact binary chosen for review.
- Match the store version to the build's `CFBundleShortVersionString` before
  selecting the build in App Store Connect.
- Complete the App Privacy questionnaire from an audit of actual data collection,
  including optional YOG accounts, chat, and shared maps. Publish a reachable
  privacy policy URL before submitting the store version.
- Assess the age-rating questions against current gameplay combat, multiplayer
  chat, and shared maps; do not answer all content questions “none” based only on
  the single-player flow.
- Confirm age rating, pricing, availability, copyright, support
  contact, and any regional compliance questions in App Store Connect.
- Set App Review sign-in to *not required* if the selected game flow needs no
  account; provide review notes describing the single-player path and optional
  network features. Keep the version on **manual release**.
- Review final screenshots and copy on the App Store Connect page, then submit
  the App Store version separately from the TestFlight beta review.
