# Globulation 2 Android privacy policy

This policy applies to the Android version of Globulation 2 (`org.globulation2.glob2`) distributed by the Globulation 2 project. For privacy questions, contact [bradley.allen.arsenault@gmail.com](mailto:bradley.allen.arsenault@gmail.com).

## Data on your device

The game saves your settings, maps, and saved games on your device so that you can continue playing. The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. Android backup is disabled for the app. You can remove locally stored game data through Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app.

## Multiplayer

LAN multiplayer and YOG online sign-in are unavailable in the current Android test build while multiplayer networking and the Globulation 2 server are being renewed. This build does not create or use an online account, and the Android app does not request Internet access. We will update this policy and the Google Play data declarations before enabling multiplayer in an Android release.

## Other network access

Opening this policy from the game launches your device's browser. That browser connects to the policy host under its own privacy terms. The game itself does not send gameplay, account, or diagnostic data off your device.

## Retention, deletion, and changes

We do not retain data from this Android build on a project-operated server. Local game data remains until you delete it from your device. For privacy questions or requests, email the contact above. We will revise this policy when Android data practices change.
