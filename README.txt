IMPORTANT NEGATIVE EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
The120 earlier native confirmations always ran baseline first due to a rotation
bug; original five-role B/W ordering was also baseline-first. Retain these raw
results, but do not assign their differences solely to W. The balanced N4
screen did not establish an N4 loss or prove a gain. Final whole-winner results
show allocation-dependent effects; concurrent throughput intervals span zero.
W reduces some measured overhead in tested contexts; it is not a free universal
whole-engine speedup. Read map/allocation-specific tables, including adverse
seeds and wall/CPU/system/context-switch distinctions.

Distributed FIFO and strict lane affinity were rejected: FIFO urgency diagnostics
showed late urgent work behind a backlog; affinity failed required independent/
ordered-lane concurrency. Adaptive last/EWMA estimates did not justify their
production complexity. Priority variants, immediate-counter removal and other
prototypes remain experiments, not incorporated W behavior. Near-full wake
heuristics were deferred after the apparent N4 loss failed to reproduce.
All selected compact negative cells, raw receipts, failed contracts, source
patches and diagnostic commands remain in the selection; favorable examples do
not replace the complete aggregate cells.
